# K24A2 donor reference (published figures, not my measurements)

- Displacement: 2354 cc, i-VTEC
- Power: 200 hp @ 6800 rpm (TSX spec)
- Torque: 166 lb-ft @ 4500 rpm
- Engine height (sump to valve cover, stock pan): 21.6 in (548.6 mm)
- Engine length (accessory to bellhousing): 24.4 in (619.8 mm)
- Oil pan: cast aluminium, front sump, approx 7.1 in deep at sump (180 mm)
- Bellhousing face to crank centerline: 3.35 in (85 mm)
- Clutch: 240 mm dual-mass (being replaced by single-mass kit)
- Engine weight (dry): 295 lb with PPO... (approx 134 kg)

Source: Honda TSX 2004 service manual spec sheet, CL swap guide cross-check.
